# Privacy Policy

Runfern does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no accounts, analytics,
advertising or tracking, and the developer runs no servers for it.

Projects, Linux environments and settings stay on your device. Runfern connects to the
internet only to download what you choose to install:

- the signed component catalog and Linux images from GitHub (github.com and its download hosts)
- editor extensions from Open VSX (open-vsx.org)
- packages and tools that you install inside the Linux environment, from their own sources

These services receive the usual technical request data, such as your IP address, under
their own privacy policies. Runfern sends them no personal information.

Contact: [GitHub issues](https://github.com/morzan1001/Runfern/issues)
